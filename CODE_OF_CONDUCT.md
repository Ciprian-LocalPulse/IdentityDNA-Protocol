# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We want IdentityDNA Protocol's community — contributors, reviewers, and researchers — to be a welcoming and harassment-free environment for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

## Expected Behavior
- Be respectful of differing viewpoints, especially in technical disagreements over protocol design.
- Give and accept constructive criticism gracefully.
- Focus critique on ideas and implementations, not people.

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language or jokes.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.

## Enforcement
Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to **contact@agentflow-enterprise.com**. All reports will be reviewed and handled with discretion.

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and in public spaces when an individual is representing the project.
